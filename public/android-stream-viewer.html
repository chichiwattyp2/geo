📋 How to Use:

  1. Click Start Viewer button above
  2. Copy your Viewer ID (shown above in purple box)
  3. Open the Android app and enter your PC's IP address
  4. The Android app will show you as an available viewer
  5. Click connect on Android to start streaming!

🔧 Troubleshooting

Can't connect? Make sure your Android device and PC are on the same network.

Find your PC's IP: Open Command Prompt/Terminal and type ipconfig (Windows) or ifconfig (Mac/Linux)

Firewall issues? Make sure port 8080 is allowed through your firewall.
